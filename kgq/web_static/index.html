Start from your own source

A .zip of a repository, or one .py, .pdf or .docx file. Files in formats with no analyser are recorded and reported, never silently dropped.

Archives are treated as hostile input: path traversal, absolute paths, symlink members, oversized members and oversized archives are refused.
