# Checkout Abandonment Study

Shoppers walk away when a purchase stalls. Our study found that once a
transaction has been pending for about half a minute, most people give up and
close the tab.

The practical conclusion: a stalled authorization should be cut loose rather
than held open. Holding a slow request does not recover the sale, and it
consumes a connection that another shopper needs.

This study informed how long the system waits before abandoning an in-flight
card charge.
